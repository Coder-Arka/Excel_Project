# Excel_Project
An interactive Excel dashboard for analyzing sales performance and profitability using KPI cards, Pivot Tables, and charts. Tracks revenue, gross profit, profit margins, orders, and COGS, with monthly trends, country and regional comparisons, sales channel analysis, and interactive slicers for data-driven business insights.

# 📊 Excel Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Performance and Profitability Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel** to analyze revenue, gross profit, profitability margins, sales volume, and geographical performance. The dashboard uses KPI cards, interactive slicers, Pivot Tables, and charts to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Revenue & Profit Analysis
- Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit from January to December.
- Uses a line chart to compare monthly performance.
- Helps identify revenue fluctuations and changes in profitability.

### 2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
- Compares revenue across five countries.
- Uses a horizontal bar chart for easy comparison.
- Helps identify high-revenue geographical markets.

### 3. Revenue Share by Sales Channel
- Displays the percentage contribution of different sales channels.
- Includes Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, and Retail Partners.
- Helps understand which channels contribute most to total revenue.

### 4. Region-Wise Comparison
- Compares performance across Central Europe, Northern Europe, Southern Europe, and Western Europe.
- Uses a doughnut chart to visualize regional distribution.

### 5. Country-Wise Revenue Map
- Visualizes geographical revenue distribution across countries.
- Helps identify markets with relatively higher and lower revenue.

### 6. Interactive Slicers
The dashboard includes three interactive filters:

- **Product Category:** Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, and Travel
- **Country:** Filter performance by individual countries
- **Quarter:** Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4

These slicers allow users to explore specific segments without manually changing the underlying data.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard supports business decisions by helping users:

- Monitor overall revenue, gross profit, and cost of goods sold.
- Identify monthly changes in revenue and profitability.
- Compare country-level sales performance.
- Evaluate the contribution of different sales channels.
- Compare regional performance and geographical revenue distribution.
- Investigate how product category and quarter affect business results.

The interactive filters make it easier to move from an overall business summary to more detailed segment-level analysis.

## ⚙️ How to Use the Project

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the Excel workbook (`.xlsx`) in Microsoft Excel.
3. Navigate to the dashboard worksheet.
4. Use the Product Category, Country, and Quarter slicers to filter the data.
5. Observe the updated KPIs and charts to analyze the selected segment.
6. Use the Pivot Table worksheet, if included, to explore the underlying summaries.

**Recommended:** Use a desktop version of Microsoft Excel with Pivot Tables, slicers, and map charts supported. Some features may behave differently in older Excel versions or alternative spreadsheet applications.
